\section{Operational data and model specification}
The controlled corpus contains 6,000 training source groups, 400 validation
and 400 test groups. Each has a base world, an effective intervention, a
semantic no-op and a numeric nuisance variant. The corpus consists of
project-generated templates, not human-authored stories. Exact duplicate base
prompts are rejected. Training uses two surface renderers; validation and test
use distinct renderers. Different renderers do not establish semantic-domain
transfer or freedom from learned lexical shortcuts.

Each source contains one to six completed key transfers, optionally a cancelled
transfer plan, and a one-time decision governed by power and permission. All
handover and prerequisite information is stipulated complete through the
decision. The continuation must describe that decision before adding later
events. It must state the current holder, required action, cabinet state and
a sufficient reason in three to five natural sentences. A valid waiting reason
need not enumerate every satisfied prerequisite. Optional later events may vary;
no unique reference wording or later opening is required.

The oracle defines power returning exactly at the decision as available.
However, the current generator does not sample equality at that boundary:
base return times are four to fifteen minutes before or after the decision,
and no-ops add one minute. Sources without permission have early power;
simultaneous power and permission failures are absent. Thus this version does
not test general conjunction reasoning or every form of semantic no-op.
A manifest-bound audit of all 6,000 training groups confirmed both omissions
in each role; this is a data-support diagnostic, not a model result. An invented
development case also keeps the required waiting action while changing power
availability when permission remains absent. Such an edit preserves the
outcome, not every prerequisite or every sufficient explanation. The existing
full-state no-op contract must not be silently extended to this case.
\pending{Before primary post-training, either limit claims to this support or
register a separate boundary/multiple-blocker challenge with a rubric accepting
all sufficient reasons. Preserve the existing locked test.}

The nuisance condition shifts an explicitly unrelated museum clock. Its
starting value is selected to match the no-op's numeric character edits.
A frozen-tokenizer audit of all 6,000 training groups additionally verified
matching BPE input lengths, token edit distances, Hamming distances,
prompt--response boundaries and invariant reference tokens. The edit's location
and causal relevance remain different. This contrast therefore tests the
specified augmentation distributions, not an isolated causal effect of
semantics under every possible control.

\subsection{General-language foundation and frozen tokenizer}
The current foundation corpus uses 300,000 TinyStories training sources and
retains the original 300 validation and 300 test sources~\cite{tiny}. The dataset
revision is \texttt{f54c09fd23315a6f9c86\allowbreak f9dc80f725de7d8f9c64}; its
recorded license is CDLA-Sharing-1.0. Exact normalized-story deduplication uses
NFKC, casefolding and collapsed whitespace; 37,282 duplicate candidates were
excluded. This does not certify absence of near duplicates or familiar plots.
TinyStories is externally sourced synthetic text, not human-authored transfer.
Earlier byte-tokenized attempts were rejected on qualitative assistant
inspection, without human scores. They remain separate negative feasibility
evidence. This replacement changes
multiple factors and is not a tokenizer-only ablation.

The frozen GPT-Neo tokenizer has 50,257 entries and is neither fitted nor
pruned. BOS, EOS and padding share ID 50,256; token zero remains valid text.
Masks use sequence lengths and ignore-index $-100$, not equality with the
padding ID. EOS is supervised and may terminate generation. A newline boundary
separates prompt and response; whole-string encoding must equal the checked
concatenation. No pretrained language-model weights are loaded.

For general next-token learning, context-1,024 windows retain each target once
per corpus pass, with state reset at each window. The encoded training corpus
has 300,169 windows and 66,859,228 targets per pass. Mechanically derived
three-sentence instruction/continuation examples provide 271,170 training rows
and 10,708,980 response targets per pass. These are transformations of existing
stories, not new independent sources or state annotations. Validation has
61,573 general-language targets and 12,192 response targets in 312 SFT rows.
Held-out source identities and test-performance restrictions are retained.

Both use a 64-dimensional embedding and tied lexical head (3,216,448
parameters), dropout zero and context 1,024. The Transformer has four heads,
pre-normalized GELU blocks and 65,536 positional parameters. The recurrent
variant projects the embedding to width 325, applies two linear-candidate
minGRU cells, and projects normalized features back to the lexical width.
It is a project factorization, not the complete original minGRU stack~\cite{mingru}.
The parameter gap is 903 (about 0.0245\%); this does not equate functional
capacity, positional representation or computational cost.

For input IDs $[B,T]$, lexical features are $[B,T,64]$ and dense logits are
$[B,T,50{,}257]$. Each recurrent cell maintains a vector $[B,325]$; Transformer
key/value caches have per-layer head dimension 16 and grow with visible context.
The training head is evaluated only at supervised positions, with equivalence
to the dense objective checked separately. Padding does not advance recurrent
state. Inputs shift by one token: a logit at position $i$ predicts $z_{i+1}$;
for response start $p$, prompt positions $i<p-1$ are ignored. Response tokens
and EOS, including the final budget-limited label, are counted explicitly.

\section{Training contract and accounting}
The bounded seed-zero foundation schedule allocates 80M general-language target
tokens followed by 20M response targets per backbone. It uses FP32 AdamW,
microbatch four, clipping at norm one, and weight decay 0.1 for matrices and zero
for biases and normalization vectors. The LM learning rate warms up over 0.5M
targets to $10^{-3}$ and decays by a cosine schedule to $10^{-4}$.
SFT starts with fresh optimizer moments, warms up over 0.1M targets to
$3\cdot10^{-4}$ and decays to $3\cdot10^{-5}$. These budgets are exposure
counts per model, not numbers of unique stories. Both seed-zero foundations
have now completed their declared schedule, with 216,366 updates and
113,197,507 input tokens each. Final and latest model tensors, identities and
statistics were checked equal; byte hashes may differ because of serialization.
This completes two of the six planned parents, not the primary study.

The proposed post-training design has four conditions, two backbones and three
training seeds: 24 exact-budget runs of 2M response targets, or 48M post targets.
It requires six genuine seed-matched foundation parents with the same corpus,
tokenizer and 80M-plus-20M schedule, counting shared physical pretraining once.
Seed-zero weights cannot be relabelled as three independent initializations.
Each arm forks its matching parent with a fresh optimizer. Additional parent
plans, measured study resources, writing review and primary registration remain
prerequisites; the post template is not an executed study.

The 2M setting is a pilot template. The earlier conditional confirmation
ceiling of 10M targets per run remains unexecuted; pilot completion alone would
not establish its replacement or scientific adequacy. Fix the confirmation
budget, stopping rule and any continuation/replay accounting after development
evidence and before final-test release. Repeating a pilot trajectory is not an
independent replication, and optimization must not be repeated until a
favorable contrast appears.

The prepared post optimizer uses FP32 AdamW at $3\cdot10^{-4}$, microbatch two,
weight decay 0.01 for matrices, and norm-one clipping. Prompt labels and surplus
labels in the final partial batch are masked. Epoch/cursor, optimizer and RNG
states, parent identity, code and input hashes are checkpointed; resume rejects
changed inputs or plans. Replayed work after an interrupted uncommitted update
must be disclosed and counted rather than hidden.

A3 cycles holder, action, cabinet, power availability and permission probes on
base/no-op pairs. Its Jensen--Shannon term, weighted by 0.1, compares aligned
teacher-forced canonical answer-token positions including EOS. Probe prefixes
and targets are train-only information; no probe CE is added. Extra input
positions, supervised CE labels, probe positions and passes are separate
counters. This component control does not reproduce an entire published
alignment or trace-editing system; A1--A2 does not depend on the extra labels.

Parameter matching, target matching and compute matching are distinct. Report
GPU model, optimizer time, full wall time, allocated/reserved VRAM, input tokens,
probe cost, selection budget and inference cost. A $6P$-per-token proxy omits
attention, optimizer and other costs and is not total FLOPs. The local host has
an RTX 5050 Laptop GPU, observed by PyTorch with 8,546,484,224 memory bytes,
and an AMD Ryzen AI 5 340 CPU; no RTX 3060 is assumed. Foundation segments have
actually run on Colab L4 and T4, with hardware/version records per segment.
This mixed-hardware trajectory is not claimed bitwise identical or cost-matched.
Local research-training throughput, complete-study credit debit and final
post-training costs remain unmeasured.

\section{Scoring, selection and inference protocol}
All future writing/primary generation uses the same canonical CPU/FP64 backend:
unchanged FP32 learned weights are promoted in a copy; forward logits and
sampling remain FP64 with MHA inference fastpath disabled. Exact top-k ties
use ascending token ID. The fixed policy is temperature 0.8, top-k 40,
maximum 128 new BPE tokens and seed 17, with EOS termination. The shared
BOS/PAD/EOS ID must remain generatable. No oracle state table is model input.
Shared seeds are a reproducibility control, not identification of an exogenous
counterfactual noise variable.

This backend was introduced after a documented FP32 top-k cutoff failure during
foundation feasibility. The old failed receipts remain failed. Fixed
same-weight full/cache/step checks use FP64 logit tolerance $10^{-10}$,
zero argmax mismatches and probability $L_1\leq10^{-4}$; tested causality and
reset checks are exact. The correction preceded primary outputs and did not
change training math, data or target budgets. It does not prove equivalence of
every generated sequence or portability across software versions. Its inference
cost and actual production environment must be reported.

Before the primary study, a fixed panel has 16 historical validation prefixes
and one unconditional three-sentence request per backbone. The prospective feasibility rule
requires all 34 real samples rated, at least 12 of 16 conditional samples per
backbone jointly satisfying format, fluency at least three out of five, prefix
adherence, a concrete new event, consistency and no ambiguity, plus a passing
unconditional sample obeying that exact three-sentence request. No group of
identical conditional outputs may exceed four after NFKC, casefolding and
whitespace normalization; raw outputs remain unchanged.
This is a descriptive engineering choice, not a validated population cutoff.
It uses the one available author-reader with limited model-ID blinding;
no independent human-validation claim follows from it. All 34 fixed FP64
outputs now exist: no empty or capped sample was observed, all emitted EOS,
and each backbone produced 16 distinct conditional outputs. These counts do
not establish format, fluency or consistency. No real panel ratings exist yet.

Final-test release requires all 24 complete post checkpoints, a fixed decoding
policy, reviewed writing/validation evidence and a hash-bound local analysis
registration. The smallest useful effect, resource cap, source cohort and
included annotation arms must be fixed before generation. A predeclared A1/A2
annotation cohort can reduce annotation effort while retaining the 24-run
training requirement. Arms outside that cohort cannot supply a matched primary
test comparison. Do not pool unmatched cohorts or call a data hash alone a
completed preregistration.

Two genuine independent blinded readers are required by the primary analysis
contract. They record proposition correctness, contradiction/type, format,
coverage, fluency, ambiguity and evidence spans. Logical disagreements require
adjudication; report pre-adjudication agreement, with kappa undefined for
appropriate degenerate marginals. Coverage and fluency average both readers
unless explicit complete adjudication supplies final scores. Invalid, empty,
unclear or capped outputs remain in the denominator. A second reader has not
been recruited; expert labels from a source dataset do not replace judgments
of newly generated continuations.

For source $s$, let $S_{s,r}=1$ when all four mandatory propositions hold,
three-to-five-sentence form is valid, and no contradiction or unresolved
ambiguity is judged for role $r$. Otherwise it is zero. The source outcome is
\[
Y_s=\tfrac12\bigl(S_{s,b}S_{s,e}+S_{s,b}S_{s,n}\bigr),
\]
where $b,e,n$ denote base, effective and no-op. The primary contrast is A1--A2
for each backbone. Resample whole sources paired across arms, retaining roles
and observed seeds. A source bootstrap interval is conditional on these seeds;
report each training seed separately rather than implying it captures all
initialization uncertainty. Report changed/preserved facts, both pair kinds,
coverage, contradiction types, fluency and generic avoidance alongside it.

General-language perplexity is $\exp(\sum_s L_s/\sum_s N_s)$, with unscaled
full-vocabulary negative log-likelihood $L_s$ and target count $N_s$, including
EOS. Multiple reset windows are summed within a story before 2,000 whole-source
bootstrap draws (seed 2026). PPL is not compared across tokenizers and is not a
logic or fluency judgment. General test text remains behind the registered
selection; foundation feasibility uses validation only.

As a secondary development diagnostic, the same 300 validation stories and
61,573 targets were scored at the 80M LM boundary and the completed 100M
parent. Transformer PPL increased from 9.40945 to 83.43866; minGRU PPL increased
from 11.56888 to 125.21978. These negative likelihood changes are retained.
The comparison is conditional on one training seed and lacks a budget-matched
continued-LM control; it does not identify a cause or measure free-generation
fluency. It neither substitutes response-only validation PPL for general PPL
nor licenses selecting a different parent after observing the outcome.

Human-authored transfer remains unresolved. Natural Stories and FairytaleQA
are candidate sources, not completed transfer datasets. FairytaleQA's pinned
metadata covers 278 stories in 15 origin-book groups; per-edition rights,
provenance, overlap, self-contained context, interventions and actual human
review are still pending~\cite{fairytaleqa}. Original QA annotations do not label
new free continuations. Any adapted prefix or intervention must be identified;
uncertainty must respect story and source-book dependence. Transfer cannot be
inferred from an unchanged synthetic template in a different surface style.

\section{Research transparency}
AI assistance supported literature discovery, protocol drafting, code, tests
and manuscript editing. An assistance ledger records those uses. The recorded
Codex application version is distinct from the underlying model identifier;
the latter must not be invented. No generated or fixture label is a human
judgment. Human authors must verify source rights, citations, code, final claims
and declarations. Reader recruitment, consent and any required institutional
ethics determination remain unresolved. No controlled test effect or editorial
outcome is available, and this draft has not been recompiled during the
user-specified evidence hold.
