\begin{abstract}
This planned study evaluates how automatic evaluators distinguish legitimate
state updates, stale-state contradictions, invariant violations, and omitted
required consequences in short autoregressive story continuations. We compare
a world/time grouped checklist with a strong flat checklist using the same
proposition information and judge backbone. Controlled edits diagnose specific
failure modes; unmodified generated prose with independent human labels supplies
the primary evaluation. The intended outcome is validated scoring infrastructure
for the later MIM architecture study, not a claim that MIM outperforms other models.

\section{Question, contribution, and relationship to Q1}
The primary question is whether grouping obligations by narrative world and time
improves semantic task-success judgments beyond a strong flat checklist, at
matched proposition information and within the same evaluator backbone.
The contribution must be an independently useful empirical account of evaluator
validity, not merely a dataset or a renamed all-requirements metric.

The three conditional Q3 studies support the main Q1 paper. Q3-A supplies a
validated task contract, rubric and frozen evaluator; Q3-B studies train-only
state supervision; Q3-C studies memory mechanisms and resource-matched controls.
Q1 tests the general value of explicit consecutive-state differencing through
MIM-N and MIM-S. Shared data, methods and earlier findings must be disclosed and
cited. Q1 needs new source stories after Q3-driven model selection. It need not
wait for the Q3 manuscripts to be accepted or published.

\section{Prior work and the bounded novelty claim}
TIME TRAVEL already studies counterfactual generation and causal
invariance~\cite{timetravel}; Counterfactual Invariance Prediction classifies
retained events~\cite{cip}. PASTA studies participant-state perturbations and
story revision~\cite{pasta}. Entity tracking~\cite{entity,naturaltracking},
ConStory-Bench~\cite{constory} and ChronoScope~\cite{chronoscope} preclude broad
novelty claims about tracking, self-history consistency or sequential temporal
scope. OpenMEVA already evaluates story metrics using human judgments and
perturbations~\cite{openmeva}. CheckEval decomposes evaluation into Boolean
checklists~\cite{checkeval}; it motivates the strong comparison arm rather than
a weak generic judge alone. FactTrack already models validity over
time~\cite{facttrack}. We must demonstrate incremental findings on the specific
controlled task and transfer to unmodified outputs.

Omission is not automatically contradiction. A contradiction detector may correctly
find no contradiction in an incomplete answer; our question is whether using it
alone as a proxy for complete task success is sufficient. External state and
repair methods such as ConWriter~\cite{conwriter} are distinct from backbone-only
generation. ScratchWorld~\cite{scratchworld}, currently a preprint, also cautions
against allowing persistent fields to dominate changed-state evaluation.

\section{Task contract and data provenance}
An input contains a narrative prefix and a request for a three-to-five-sentence
continuation, generated autoregressively as
\(p_\theta(y\mid x)=\prod_t p_\theta(y_t\mid x,y_{<t})\).
State \(s^w_{t,k}\) is indexed by world \(w\), time \(t\) and micro-event order \(k\).
Successful transfers update possession. Agents obey the stated rule, all prefix
transfers precede the decision, and no alternative action defeats its premise.
A point-in-time rule is evaluated once; exclude exact-boundary events initially
or specify their order. An obligation alone does not imply execution without
the compliance assumption.

\begin{quote}\small
Illustration, not model output: Lan passes the key to Huy, who passes it to Mai
before 9:00. Everyone obeys the rule: at exactly 9:00 the holder opens the locked
cabinet if power has returned, otherwise waits. Power returns at 8:55. Changing
only 8:55 to 9:05 changes the required action and lock status at 9:00, but not
the earlier transfer history or possession immediately before the action.
The one-time rule does not uniquely require opening at 9:05. A ten-minute delay
relative to the 9:05 premise means 9:15.
\end{quote}

Core scope is one point-in-time trigger schema across three proposed contexts:
access to storage, parcel handover, and equipment preparation. These are not
three independent logical mechanisms. Include effective and no-op interventions,
depth and recency controls, irrelevant mentions and shortcut anchors. The existing
cabinet v0 has fixed times and distinct recipients; it is a smoke benchmark and
does not implement the revised protocol.

\section{Human gold, primary outcome, and uncertainty}
Two blinded readers independently mark required propositions, contradictions,
unclear cases and evidence spans. Keep pre-adjudication labels, disagreements,
prevalence and reader time. Train the rubric only on development. Neither an
oracle nor agreement alone establishes free-text validity. Audit controlled edits
for unintended second errors; the primary sample remains unmodified generated
prose, not hand-selected mistakes.
Adjudicate unclear human cases before viewing evaluator results. Report unresolved
cases and sensitivity bounds rather than silently dropping them; withhold the
primary claim if gold cannot be established reliably.

Gold semantic success is \(y_i=1\) when all mandatory propositions are correctly
expressed without contradiction in the applicable world/time. Fluency and sentence
format are separate secondary outcomes. The proposed primary estimand is
\[
 \Delta_{BA}=BA(E2)-BA(E1),\qquad
 BA(E)=\frac12\left(\frac{TP}{n_{y=1}}+\frac{TN}{n_{y=0}}\right).
\]
Abstentions and parse failures remain in their gold-class denominator and receive
no true-positive/true-negative credit. If a class is absent, report BA as undefined;
do not fill the class by selecting favorable test outputs. Report source-provenance
strata and generator families separately as well as the declared aggregate.

Resample source clusters jointly for E1/E2, retaining both premises and all
generator outputs, and compute a 95\% interval for the paired difference.
Judge backbones and decoding replications are reported separately; sentences
are not independent samples. Register secondary comparisons and distinguish
exploratory analyses. A proposed smallest effect of interest is five BA percentage
points, to be confirmed from feasibility before registration; it is not a journal
requirement. Failure to reject a difference does not establish equivalence.

Mandatory diagnostics include false acceptance/rejection, valid-update false
alarms, stale-state and world-mixing detection, omission, changed/invariant facts,
format, fluency, abstention and annotation agreement. A new aggregate that rewards
coverage by construction is not independent evidence of validity.
