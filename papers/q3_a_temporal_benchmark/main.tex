\begin{abstract}
This proposed study tests whether semantic no-op interventions teach compact
autoregressive language models when a changed premise should leave required
story consequences unchanged. We compare effective-only pairs, effective/no-op
pairs, matched numeric nuisance controls, and a train-only invariance component.
The primary outcome is joint success on changed and preserved propositions in
held-out multi-sentence continuations. The design separates useful selectivity
from lexical augmentation, copying and generic avoidance.

\section{Question and prior work}
Counterfactual story rewriting is established in TIME TRAVEL~\cite{timetravel};
PASTA includes story revision under counterfactual participant states~\cite{pasta}.
DTO optimizes rewriting through a differentiable objective~\cite{dto}.
Task-specific objectives and evaluation metrics for counterfactual rewriting
are also studied by Girard et al.~\cite{girard_tallip}.

Counterfactual-invariance alignment uses matched prompts and a group-level
policy-gradient reward~\cite{cfi}. CSR studies verifier-approved trace edits and
reports target accuracy, coverage and null-edit preservation alongside
sensitivity~\cite{csr}. It also includes narrative evaluations, so narrative
outputs or a JS term alone do not establish novelty here. The narrower question
is whether training on semantic no-op changes
improves consequence selectivity beyond matched lexical augmentation, including
transfer outside the training templates. Invariance tests inherit behavioral
testing principles~\cite{checklist}.

\section{Interventions and training conditions}
An effective intervention changes a required consequence; a semantic no-op
changes a premise without changing that consequence. It need not preserve every
fact in the world. Changing power return from 8:50 to 8:55 before a 9:00 decision
preserves the required action, whereas 8:55 to 9:05 changes it. The return time
itself changes in both cases and may be correctly mentioned in the prose.
Preserved propositions are time/world indexed, not permanent facts.

The primary contrast is A1 minus A2; A0 and A3 are secondary comparisons.
Match token exposure, source/event complexity, length and edit-distance
distributions where possible, and report residual differences. Reweight or
resample within a fixed source pool rather than giving A1 more training targets.
Include named shortcuts (last-recipient, clock-only, always-change and no-change)
to audit the development data. Keep complete template/paraphrase families and
source stories outside training.

The operational control shifts an explicitly unrelated museum clock by one
minute. Its independent starting time is sampled conditional on matching the
power-time no-op's byte length and number of edited numeric characters. All
conditions include the same background-clock premise; only this control changes
it. Decision targets remain unchanged. Edit location and causal relevance still
differ, so this does not identify a pure semantic effect. Require transfer beyond
clock templates. This control replaces the earlier punctuation-only preparation.

For A3, match next-token distributions only on canonical factual-probe answers
known to be invariant, with a fixed continuation prefix. A schematic penalty is
\(\lambda |U|^{-1}\sum_{j\in U}\operatorname{JS}(q_j,q'_j)\), where U contains
audited unchanged obligations. Do not force an entire open-ended continuation
to be identical: wording, timing mentions and optional plot choices can differ.
This is a limited component control. It does not reproduce the full alignment
or trace-editing methods above. Declare extra auxiliary labels/passes and cost;
the primary A1--A2 contrast does not depend on them.

\section{Limitations and completion criteria}
The contribution must go beyond the tautology that no-op training improves
no-op test items. It needs selective transfer or a generalizable failure
principle after lexical and data-quality controls. If the effect is confined to
a single template or explained by extra exposure, narrow the conclusion.
No new invariance algorithm or causal-discovery ability is established by this
design. The current generator also leaves exact deadline equality and multiple
blocking conditions untested. Discuss annotation ambiguity, small-model writing limits, English-only
scope and corpus licensing. No trained test outputs or human reliability statistic is yet
available. Freeze the primary analysis before opening the final test, report
negative outcomes and publish actual author, consent and data/code declarations.
