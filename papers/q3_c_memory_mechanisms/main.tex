\begin{abstract}
This planned study tests the provenance of histories used to train state-consistent
continuation. We compare teacher histories, frozen peer-model histories,
histories from the learner's own frozen initialization, and a fixed teacher/self
mixture. Training targets respect events actually written in the prefix.
The primary comparison isolates self-origin versus peer-origin histories after
source, event type, difficulty and validity controls. Evaluation includes both
fixed-history continuation and free-running generation with verbatim frozen
first segments, with explicit accounting for generic avoidance and parser failure.

\section{Question and prior work}
Distribution shift under model-produced histories is established in imitation
learning~\cite{dagger}, scheduled sampling~\cite{scheduled} and training/sampling
dynamics~\cite{professor}. StateWise already combines typed-state supervision,
filtered self-training and preferences against relapse~\cite{statewise}.
FactTrack addresses time-aware facts~\cite{facttrack}, while ConStory-Bench
evaluates self-generated narrative consistency~\cite{constory}.
We do not claim these tasks or recipes as new. The question is whether the
source of a frozen history bank matters beyond its quality and difficulty,
when training and scoring honor the actual event the model chose.
Recognition of model-origin histories and surprise-dependent output entropy
already have direct evidence~\cite{enaction}; assistant-history interventions
also change later behavior~\cite{historyeffects}. Neither an entropy decrease
nor origin recognition alone establishes improved semantic memory.

\section{History banks and four training conditions}
Freeze initialization checkpoints and history banks before post-training.
``Self-origin'' refers to the learner's initial checkpoint, not its changing
policy after updates. This experiment is not an on-policy or DAgger algorithm.
An online-refresh extension would require separate identification and cost controls.

For example, a first segment may put a key in a drawer or a bag. The suffix
target must honor that actual choice, rather than substitute a teacher's old
intention. In controlled training data, only unambiguous audited events receive
oracle/parser labels. Record rejection, parse and validity rates by source;
never select only attractive self outputs while retaining all peer outputs.
Match source stories, event types, update counts, length, semantic difficulty
and coverage before comparing provenance. If matching fails, report the
confound and do not claim a causal provenance effect.
Also match or stratify frozen-learner history surprisal, with overlap and
acceptance yield reported. Lack of overlap limits identification rather than
licensing selective removal of unfavorable histories. Separate logical
consistency changes from entropy or style shifts.

Use identical target-token and selection caps across arms. All generation,
filtering and suffix-label construction costs are logged separately. A checker
that produces labels cannot validate itself: audit its reading against independent
human judgments, including naturally generated errors, negation and ambiguity.
The eventual free-text evaluation must retain items the training parser cannot
read, with unclear-case handling rather than a favorable restricted denominator.

\section{Primary outcome and two evaluation panels}
In the free-run panel, the model writes a first segment, which is fixed as raw
text and token IDs before a further continuation. Let E mean that the first
segment introduces a clear new persistent state-changing event. The primary
unconditional outcome is success on the required continuation with E, valid
prefix behavior and no contradiction. No-event and generic outputs receive no
memory-success credit. An initially incorrect event is not repaired merely by
remaining consistent with it later. The primary contrast is C2 minus C1.

A shared, frozen-history panel gives every arm the same source-specific first
segment and separates continuation errors from differences in initial writing.
Report event-creation rate, conditional consistency among valid new events,
unconditional pipeline success, fluency, ambiguity and coverage. Reading the
whole visible history establishes behavioral use of context, not a proof that
a particular hidden recurrent state stores the fact.

\section{Limitations and completion criteria}
Self-generated data alone is not a new method. A contribution requires a
provenance-specific finding or failure principle beyond data quality, parser
selection and format effects. If warm-up models cannot produce clear events,
stop at feasibility rather than relabel teacher prose as self-generated.
Discuss changes in learner distribution after the frozen bank, limited model
families, selection bias, English-only scope and human annotation cost. No
history bank, labels or new training outcome has been collected for this design.
Report negative results and incomplete matching; publish genuine authorship,
licenses, consent and tool disclosures from actual records.
