\begin{abstract}
This proposed study compares controlled histories from a scripted reference,
a frozen peer and the learner's frozen initialization when training continuation
to honor newly written events. A token-wise grammar mask fixes semantic and
syntactic eligibility in the training pilot; it does not turn those histories
into unconstrained prose. Matching uses the same source and event, length and
frozen-learner surprisal, with rejection and identical-history rates reported.
Main evaluation is unconstrained: a first segment is frozen verbatim before
continuation, including failures to create a valid event. The primary question
concerns roll-in input distributions, not an intrinsic origin label hidden
from the model.

\section{Question, identification and prior work}
DAgger\cite{dagger}, scheduled sampling\cite{scheduled} and Professor
Forcing\cite{professor} already study model-produced histories or training/
sampling dynamics. StateWise uses typed-state supervision, filtered trajectories
and preference learning\cite{statewise}. Model-origin recognition and
surprise-dependent output entropy have direct prior evidence\cite{enaction};
history interventions also affect later behavior\cite{historyeffects}.
FactTrack\cite{facttrack} and ConStory-Bench\cite{constory} motivate temporal
facts and self-generated consistency. We do not claim any of these as new.

The experimental factor changes input distributions coupled to initialization.
An origin label is not supplied to the learner. If two banks contain identical
input/target sequences in identical order under the same initialization and
randomness, the optimization updates coincide; metadata alone cannot cause an
advantage. Matching therefore does not identify a magical intrinsic ``self''
effect. It defines a narrower common-support training comparison whose value
must be demonstrated outside the controlled support.

\section{Controlled sources and genuine model roll-ins}
The prepared pool has 600/60/120 train/validation/test source groups. A prefix
tracks a key through successful transfers to a current holder, without choosing
storage. Nine allowed three-sentence histories combine three storage places
and three surface realizations; every history records a completed placement
and its persistence. This training grammar is compiled from the known holder: it is explicit
train-time state scaffolding, not evidence that a model inferred the holder.
Main inference receives neither this mask nor a gold state table.
Full-string grammar recognition gives a sound state label
within that finite support. It is not a free-text parser accuracy claim.

Teacher histories are scripted controlled references, not human annotations
or paid teacher-model outputs. Self and peer histories are sampled one token
at a time from the frozen model with allowed-token masking:
\[
q_\theta(v\mid u,G)=
\frac{p_\theta(v\mid u)\mathbf1[v\in A_G(u)]}
{\sum_{a\in A_G(u)}p_\theta(a\mid u)}.
\]
The grammar fixes support; the model supplies conditional probabilities and
sampling choices. This distribution differs from unconstrained generation.
Bank collection requires completed, hash-verified initializations from the
declared warm-up, never a random model or a relabeled teacher fixture.
No production self/peer bank exists before those real checkpoints exist.

\section{Matching and actual-event targets}
Require the same source and chosen location for self and peer. Match frozen-
learner mean surprisal, measured in nats per byte including EOS, within 0.25,
and history length within 32 bytes. The reference chooses a predetermined
surface style for that same event. The pilot requires at least 30 matched
sources per learner initialization and some nonidentical self/peer text;
otherwise no final training bank is released. These are feasibility rules,
not a power calculation. Fix matching rules before outcome inspection.

Report every offered source, event mismatch, caliper rejection and identical
history. Do not keep only favorable self outputs. Matching defines an overlap
subset and cannot establish a total population effect of provenance; scalar
surprisal does not match every hidden or stylistic property. Lack of overlap
requires stopping or revising the design, not relaxing limits until gains appear.

Suffix targets follow the event actually recorded: drawer histories receive
drawer-consistent suffixes, not a teacher's old basket intention. Each arm for
a given learner uses the same matched source/target sequence; only the visible
history changes. C3 alternates reference and self histories with any one-item
imbalance reported. Teacher-forced suffix CE masks the entire prompt/history;
no gold table or origin tag enters main inference. Checkpoint/input hashes
bind the bank to its exact learner initialization before training and resume.

\section{Unconstrained two-stage and common-history evaluation}
For the primary panel, a post-trained model writes a first segment without a
grammar mask, capped at 256 new byte tokens. Freeze its raw emitted IDs and
verbatim text, then append a fixed question asking a friend to locate the key.
Generate a three-to-five-sentence continuation with a 384-token cap. Do not
decode/re-encode an invalid byte sequence or replace the first segment with a
teacher choice. Record invalid/ambiguous text, generic responses, novel events
and initial prefix violations; none are silently filtered.

Human readers derive the actual new event from the full visible history.
Optional valid places and wording outside the training grammar remain valid.
Let E be a requested new persistent event, V prefix validity, R a correct and
history-consistent follow-up, and F the required format. Unconditional pipeline
success requires \(E\land V\land R\land F\), all mandatory propositions and
no contradiction. Consistency conditional on valid new events is secondary
with its denominator reported. Remaining consistent with an initial error
does not repair that error. A model avoiding events cannot gain memory credit.

A secondary common-history panel gives every model the same controlled first
segment for a source, separating initial writing variation from continuation
use. The primary comparison is C2 minus C1 in the free panel. Never combine
the constrained/common panel with free outcomes to hide free-generation failure.
Blinded judgments retain parser failures and uncertainty; exact grammar checks
are only training eligibility, not the primary free-text scorer.

\section{Completion criteria and limitations}
Finite-support histories, single storage events, small models and matched-subset
selection restrict scope. Constraints guarantee eligible grammar, not free
writing ability; bank generation and NLL matching cost are not free training
data. The decisive evidence is a finding or failure principle that survives
unconstrained continuation, a common-history control, seed/backbone variation,
coverage/fluency controls and licensed naturalistic transfer. Learning nine
forms or reducing entropy alone is insufficient.

Stop or merge the study if banks collapse, overlap is insufficient, free prose
fails, or data quality/style explains the result without a further insight.
No new self/peer production bank, optimization, human labels or scientific
finding has been produced during preparation. Prepared teacher contract
fixtures are not self-origin data. General chat ability and editorial outcomes
remain unestablished; report negative results and actual reuse/licenses/authorship.
